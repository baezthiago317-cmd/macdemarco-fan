03 / his story

“Some music just feels like an old friend.”

That's the feeling I get from Mac's music: a little imperfect, a little nostalgic, and somehow right for an ordinary day.

Mac DeMarco is a Canadian singer-songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, and producer known for his laid-back delivery, distinctive guitar sound, home-recorded textures, and offbeat humour. He was born in Duncan, British Columbia, and grew up in Edmonton, Alberta.

Before his solo career, he played in the band Makeout Videotape. In 2012, he released Rock and Roll Night Club and 2, introducing listeners to a relaxed, playful sound that would keep evolving throughout his career.

Salad Days (2014) brought his music to a much wider audience. Its warm guitars and reflective writing helped make it a defining record for many listeners. He followed it with Another One (2015), This Old Dog (2017), and Here Comes the Cowboy (2019).

In 2023, he released the instrumental road record Five Easy Hot Dogs and the sprawling collection One Wayne G. Guitar arrived in 2025, with a more stripped-back sound built around voice and guitar. His official Bandcamp is also the best place to explore his releases and demos.

What many listeners connect with isn't only one song. It's the atmosphere: warm guitars, strange little details, humour, honesty, and the feeling that music doesn't need to be perfect to mean something.

This page is one small fan's way of saying thank you. No grand expectations — just genuine appreciation from a long way away.

07 / a message from far away

A letter from
the other side of the world.

Hey Mac,

I'm writing this from Uruguay, a small country in South America. I made this little website because I wanted to try something different instead of just hoping you'd somehow see a comment online.

Your music has found its way into my life, and I wanted to say thank you. It's pretty amazing how a song made on the other side of the world can become part of an ordinary day here.

I appreciate the music, the atmosphere, the little details, and the personality that comes through in your work. I don't think every song needs to be perfect or explain everything. Sometimes it's enough for a song to make a moment feel a little different.

I don't expect anything from this. It would just be amazing if you happened to come across this page someday and knew that someone all the way over here appreciates what you do.

Thanks for the records, the memories, and the music that keeps finding its way into people's lives. I hope you're doing well and enjoying whatever comes next.

Take care, Mac. And seriously, thank you for everything.

— a fan from Uruguay 🇺🇾
