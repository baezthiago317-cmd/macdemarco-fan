02 / his story

“Some music just feels like an old friend.”

And that's the feeling I get from Mac's music. A little imperfect, a little nostalgic, and somehow exactly what you need on an ordinary day.

Mac DeMarco is a Canadian singer-songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, and producer, born on November 30, 1990, in Duncan, British Columbia, Canada. Known for his laid-back personality, distinctive guitar sound, and wonderfully offbeat sense of humour, he has built a musical world that feels unmistakably his own.

Before becoming known as a solo artist, he played in the Vancouver-based band Makeout Videotape. In 2012, he released Rock and Roll Night Club and 2, introducing listeners to his relaxed sound and playful, lo-fi approach to recording.

His 2014 album Salad Days helped bring his music to a much wider audience. Songs from that era captured a particular feeling: growing up, looking back, figuring things out, and trying not to take everything too seriously.

He continued exploring different sounds with Another One, This Old Dog, Here Comes the Cowboy, Five Easy Hot Dogs, and the enormous collection One Wayne G. His later work, including Guitar, shows another side of an artist who has never seemed particularly interested in standing still.

What makes Mac special to many listeners isn't just a particular song or album. It's the atmosphere: warm guitars, strange little details, humour, honesty, and the feeling that music doesn't have to be perfect to mean something.

This page is only one small fan's way of saying thank you for that. No grand expectations — just genuine appreciation from a long way away.

05 / a message from far away

A letter from
the other side of the world.

Hey Mac,

I'm writing this from Uruguay, a small country in South America. I made this little website because I wanted to try something different instead of just hoping you'd somehow see a comment online.

Your music has found its way into my life, and I wanted to say thank you. It's pretty amazing how a song made on the other side of the world can become part of an ordinary day here.

I appreciate the music, the atmosphere, the little details, and the personality that comes through in your work. I don't think every song needs to be perfect or explain everything. Sometimes it's enough for a song to make a moment feel a little different.

I don't expect anything from this. It would just be amazing if you happened to come across this page someday and knew that someone all the way over here appreciates what you do.

Thanks for the records, the memories, and the music that keeps finding its way into people's lives. I hope you're doing well and enjoying whatever comes next.

Take care, Mac. And seriously, thank you for everything.

— a fan from Uruguay 🇺🇾
