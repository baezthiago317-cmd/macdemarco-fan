Hey Mac,

I'm writing this from Uruguay, a small country in South America. I made this little website because I wanted to try something different instead of just hoping you'd somehow see a comment on the internet.

Your music has found its way into my life, and I wanted to say thank you. It's pretty cool how a song made by someone on the other side of the world can become part of an ordinary day here.

I don't expect anything from this. It would just be amazing if you happened to come across this page someday and knew that someone all the way over here appreciates what you do.

Hope you're doing well. Thanks for making music.
